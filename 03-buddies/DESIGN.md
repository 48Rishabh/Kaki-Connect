## Brand & Style

This design system embraces a contemporary, minimalist Korean stationery and lifestyle aesthetic. It harmonizes structured, airy graphic layouts with whimsical, charming micro-moments. The emotional tone is calm, reassuring, softly joyful, and uncluttered—designed to feel like a thoughtfully curated stationery journal, aesthetic desk toy collection, or cozy digital sanctuary.

### Design Movement: Warm Soft-Minimalism with Playful Post-Modern Touches
- **Purity of Space:** Expansive breathing room, crisp visual alignment, and precise typography grounded on comforting, warm off-white foundations rather than sterile tech-gray tones.
- **Graphic Whimsy:** Gentle geometric play—wavy optical contours, rounded squircle frames, micro-checkered motifs, stylized smiley faces, and light mid-century retro vector wireframes.
- **Serene Dual-Tone Contrast:** Restrained contrast relying predominantly on periwinkle cornflower sky blue paired with optimistic sunlight yellow.
- **Tactile Warmth:** UI components resemble velvety matte cards, sticker sheets, and smooth ceramic tokens with zero harsh visual boundaries.

## Colors

The palette is tuned around tactile, cheerful pastel harmony, avoiding stark digital primaries in favor of velvety, natural stationery inks.

### Primary Role (Cornflower Sky Blue - `#638ECB`)
The foundational anchor across key actions, selected states, and prominent graphic cards. It carries a restful, reassuring weight while remaining buoyant. Deep accents extend down to `#3E6DAE`, while gentle tint washes hover at `#EAF1FA`.

### Secondary Role (Sunshine Yellow - `#F4D35E`)
A soft citrus pop used for delight markers, positive tags, smiley graphic highlights, and energetic accents. Supported by a creamy pastel butter tone (`#FEF7DC`) and an amber ochre (`#D9AC28`) for sharp legibility when needed.

### Tertiary Role (Airy Periwinkle Sky - `#89B4E9`)
Softens gradients, backdrop cards, and decorative background geometry. Acts as a bridging hue between deep cornflower and neutral whites.

### Neutral Foundation & Canvas (`#F5F3EF`)
- **Canvas Base:** Soft milky alabaster/bone white (`#F5F3EF`) that prevents eye fatigue.
- **Card Surface:** Pure warm white (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle cream undertones.
- **Typography & Details:** Crisp off-charcoal (`#22262F`) for headers and smooth slate ink (`#666D7E`) for secondary bilingual captions, ensuring balanced contrast without harsh black collision.

## Typography

The typography uses **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its friendly geometric curves, pristine modern counters, and calm legibility.

### Hierarchy & Style Rules
- **Display & Headlines:** Set with tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and medium-to-semibold weights. Headlines never shout; they guide gently.
- **Bilingual & Subtitle Layouts:** Frequent pairing of an English title in `headline-sm` with a soft Hangul/Korean descriptor directly underneath in `caption-hangul` with secondary ink coloring (`#8B93A7`), replicating modern Seoul lifestyle packaging.
- **Numerics & Grid Data:** Clean tabular or monospaced alignments for puzzle layouts, counters, and playful cards with relaxed leading.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a flexible grid anchored on a rhythmic 8px spacing cadence, emphasizing generous negative space to cultivate a relaxing, gallery-like browsing experience.

### Grid Architecture
- **Mobile (<768px):** 2-column or single-column fluid grid. Gutters set to `16px`, margins to `20px`. Cards display compact aspect ratios (4:5 or 3:4) with generous internal padding (`16px`).
- **Tablet (768px–1024px):** 3 to 4-column balanced grid with `20px` gutters and `32px` page margins.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** 4 to 6-column card matrix conforming to a maximum content width of `1200px`. Gutters sit at `24px` with dynamic outer margins.

### Spatial Rhythms
- **Internal Card Framing:** Content within cards maintains a minimum edge offset of `20px`, reserving space around central illustrations or patterns to replicate collectable art cards.
- **Vertical Air:** Sections and distinct category blocks are separated by `spacing-2xl` to `spacing-3xl` (48px - 64px), ensuring elements do not feel crowded.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids dark dropshadows, synthetic 3D glows, and heavy multi-layered z-depth. Visual elevation is achieved via **tonal stacking, gentle ambient illumination, and crisp boundary contrasts**.

### Depth Layers
- **Ground Floor (Base Canvas):** Warm eggshell `#F5F3EF`.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Panels):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` or solid cornflower blue `#638ECB`. Cards gain depth through an ultra-soft, diffused perimeter aura: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(99, 142, 203, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(34, 38, 47, 0.03);`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Pills & Overlays):** Interactive floating action pills and popovers use a crisp dual-shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(99, 142, 203, 0.16)`.
- **Subsurface Outlines:** Surfaces may feature a hair-thin tone-on-tone border (`1px solid rgba(99, 142, 203, 0.12)` or `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`) to ensure separation against neighboring pale components without harshness.

## Shapes

The visual signature is driven by comfortable, squircle-inspired curves that convey warmth, tactility, and approachable craftsmanship.

### Radius Architecture
- **Interactive Buttons & Badges:** Full stadium pill styling (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) for high playfulness and clickability.
- **Cards & Primary Modules:** `rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl` (16px to 24px radius, scaled with card proportion). This produces an organic squircle silhouette reminiscent of curved Korean stationery pads.
- **Inputs & Text Fields:** Smooth `rounded-xl` (12px to 14px) offering a balanced middle ground between the structured layout and the rounded pills.
- **Graphic Motifs:** Perfect circles for smiley stamps, organic free-form pebbles for accent artwork, and pilloid tags.

## Components

### 1. Cards (The Core Signature)
- **Aspect Ratio & Canvas:** Tall portrait squircle tiles (3:4 or 4:5 ratio) with clean rounded corners (`20px`).
- **Header Section:** Top-aligned title in `headline-sm`, complemented by secondary Hangul caption underneath (`caption-hangul`). Top-right corner houses minimalist diagonal accents (`//`) or subtle functional glyphs.
- **Canvas Interior:** Features ample white or sky blue space with centered playful geometry—such as soft checkerboard matrixes, dual-tone concentric spring rings, minimalist smiley faces, or stylized pastel objects.

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid cornflower blue background (`#638ECB`), crisp white text, pill shape (`9999px`), with `12px 24px` padding. Subtle scale down (`scale(0.97)`) on press.
- **Secondary / Playful:** Sunshine yellow (`#F4D35E`) with warm dark slate text (`#22262F`).
- **Ghost / Minimal:** Transparent container with `1.5px` border in `#638ECB` and matching text color.

### 3. Chips & Dual-Tone Badges
- **Style:** Compact pill tags featuring a split-tint or two-tone visual motif (e.g., butter yellow paired with a periwinkle dot or label).
- **Structure:** `padding: 4px 12px;`, `label-sm` font, soft pastel background (`#EAF1FA` or `#FEF7DC`), with high-contrast text.

### 4. Input Fields
- **Container:** Warm white base, `rounded-xl` (12px), bordered by a calm, low-contrast border (`#E2DFD7`).
- **Focus State:** Glow-free crisp boundary shift to `#638ECB` with an ethereal ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(99, 142, 203, 0.15)`).
- **Placeholder:** Subdued slate stone (`#A4ACB9`).

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Radio Buttons:** Circular with generous inset dot when selected, styled in `#638ECB` over `#FFFFFF`.
- **Checkboxes:** Rounded squares (`6px` radius). When active, filled with `#638ECB` carrying a bold, friendly white checkmark icon.

### 6. Playful Geometric Accents & Patterns
- **Soft Checkerboard:** Subtle alternating tiles of pale cornflower blue and butter cream yellow.
- **Smileys & Glyphs:** Simple circular face icons with curved black arc smiles and clean circular eye dots.
- **Retro Line Art:** 1.5px continuous-line minimalist wireframes (slanted pencils, slinky rings, concentric maze paths) rendered with balanced negative space.
